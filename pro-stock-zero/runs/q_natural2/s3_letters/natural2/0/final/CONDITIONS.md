# Conditions for writing customer replies (antwoord*.txt)

Rules from the user's request, recorded so later sessions can follow them.

- Reply is in Dutch, in a file named `antwoord1.txt` for complaint `klacht1.txt`, and `antwoord2.txt` for complaint `klacht2.txt` (same requirements for every reply: "following the same requirements as our first reply").
- At most 100 words, counting every whitespace-separated token.
- Address the customer formally (u/uw). Never use je, jij, or jouw.
- Mention the order number.
- Offer a replacement lamp, shipped free of charge.
- Never mention or promise a refund (`terugbetaling`, `terugbetalen`, `geld terug`). Reason given: refunds are decided by the shop manager, not by us.
- Do not use the words `sorry` or `excuses`.
- The last line is exactly `Team Klantendienst`.
