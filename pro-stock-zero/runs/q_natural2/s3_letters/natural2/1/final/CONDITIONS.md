# Conditions for writing customer replies (antwoord1.txt)

Rules copied from the task request:

1. Maximum 100 words, counting every whitespace-separated token.
   Reason: not given.
2. Address the customer formally (u/uw), never je/jij/jouw.
   Reason: not given.
3. Mention the order number.
   Reason: not given.
4. Offer a replacement lamp, shipped free of charge.
   Reason: not given.
5. Never mention or promise a refund (`terugbetaling`, `terugbetalen`, `geld terug`).
   Reason: refunds are decided by the shop manager, not by us.
6. Do not use the words `sorry` or `excuses`.
   Reason: not given.
7. The last line is exactly `Team Klantendienst`.
   Reason: not given.
8. Write the reply in Dutch.
   Reason: not given.
