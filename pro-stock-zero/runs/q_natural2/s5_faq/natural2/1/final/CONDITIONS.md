# Conditions for the FAQ task

Source: user request to write `faq.md` from the customer questions in `vragen.txt`. No reasons were given for these rules.

1. Each entry is a question on its own line in bold (`**...?**`), followed on the next line by its answer, then a blank line.
2. Entries are sorted alphabetically by question.
3. Each answer has at most 25 words.
4. Address the reader formally (u/uw), never je/jij/jouw.
5. Never state a price, amount or percentage: refer to the price list at `winkel.be/prijzen` instead.
