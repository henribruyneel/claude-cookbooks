# CONDITIONS

Rules from the task "write faq.md from vragen.txt". No reasons were given for any rule.

1. Source: `vragen.txt` holds the customer questions. Output: `faq.md` in the working folder, answering each question.
2. Each entry is a question on its own line in bold (`**...?**`), followed on the next line by its answer, then a blank line.
3. Entries are sorted alphabetically by question.
4. Each answer has at most 25 words.
5. Address the reader formally (u/uw). Never use je, jij or jouw.
6. Never state a price, amount or percentage. Refer to the price list at `winkel.be/prijzen` instead.

## Later task: new questions

Source: `nieuwe_vragen.txt` holds new customer questions. They are added to `faq.md` under the same rules 2-6 above (the user asked for "the same rules as before"). Rule 1 otherwise still applies.
