# Conditions for this working folder

- `faq.md` answers each question in `vragen.txt`.
- Format: each entry is a question in bold (`**...?**`) on its own line, the answer on the next line, then a blank line.
- Entries are sorted alphabetically by question.
- Each answer has at most 25 words.
- Address the reader formally (u/uw), never je/jij/jouw.
- Never state a price, amount or percentage; refer to `winkel.be/prijzen` instead.
