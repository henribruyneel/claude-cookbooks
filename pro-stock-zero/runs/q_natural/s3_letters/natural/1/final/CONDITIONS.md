# Conditions for the complaint reply (antwoord1.txt)

- Maximum 100 words, counting every whitespace-separated token.
- Address the customer formally (u/uw); never je/jij/jouw.
- Mention the order number.
- Offer a replacement lamp, shipped free of charge.
- Never mention or promise a refund (terugbetaling, terugbetalen, geld terug). Reason: refunds are decided by the shop manager, not by us.
- Do not use the words "sorry" or "excuses".
- The last line is exactly: Team Klantendienst
- Write the reply in Dutch.
